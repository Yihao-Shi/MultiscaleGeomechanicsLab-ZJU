        ## Fundamental Soil Mechanics & Computational Geomechanics
        
        The Multiscale Geomechanics Lab focuses on fundamental soil mechanics and computational geomechanics. We are enthusiastic about developing advanced numerical methods, including:
        
        - **Multiscale Methods** - Bridging scales from particle to continuum
        - **Meshless Methods** - Flexible and efficient computational approaches
        - **Physics-Informed Data-Driven Methods** - Integrating machine learning with physics
        
        These methods are applied to model geomaterials and geostructures, advancing the understanding of complex soil behavior and geotechnical engineering challenges.
